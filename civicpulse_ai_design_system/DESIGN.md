## Brand & Style

This design system drives a next-generation civic innovation platform engineered for nationwide hackathons, multi-stakeholder governance, academic labs, and corporate tech mentorship. 

### Brand Narrative & Emotion
The platform must project authority, momentum, intellectual rigor, and transparent democracy. It rejects the archaic bureaucracy of legacy government portals, replacing it with the precision, velocity, and sleek density found in modern software engineering platforms (e.g., Linear, Raycast) blended with high-credibility institutional clarity. Users must feel empowered, accountable, and inspired by actionable real-world impact.

### Target Audience
- **Student Innovators & Researchers:** Need fast, high-density discovery flows, clear requirements, and contextual AI insights without visual friction.
- **University Faculty & Evaluators:** Demand rapid triage workflows, clear assessment matrices, and intuitive status tracking.
- **Industry Mentors & Corporate Sponsors:** Expect executive-grade polish, clean analytics, seamless feedback loops, and intuitive navigation.
- **Civic Administrators & Nodal Officers:** Require trust-inducing, structured, high-legibility overviews of civic challenges and scalable pipelines.

### Design Archetype: "Civic-Tech Precision"
A modern blend of **Corporate Precision SaaS** and **Hyper-Refined Glass & Layering**:
- **Ultra-crisp structures:** High-legibility typography, deliberate 1px hairline borders, systematic density tiers, and disciplined whitespace.
- **Intelligent accents:** Controlled violet and indigo radiance denoting AI-synthesized intelligence against neutral, distraction-free backdrops.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for high-contrast accessibility, institutional trust, and high-velocity workflow triage.

### Core Canvas & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`background`):** `#F7F8FC` — A cool-tinted, ultra-clean off-white delivering separation from pure white cards.
- **Surface Elevation (`surface-card`):** `#FFFFFF` — Pure white for container cards, data-tables, and dialogue panels.
- **Subtle Fill (`surface-subtle`):** `#F1F3F9` — Input backgrounds, secondary toolbars, and inactive tab tracks.
- **Hairline Border (`border-default`):** `#E2E8F0` — Subtle 1px boundaries preserving interface geometry without visual weight.
- **Active Border (`border-active`):** `#CBD5E1` — Dynamic focus rings and interactive states.

### Brand & Intelligence Accents
- **Primary Brand (`primary`):** `#3155D9` — Deep royal indigo conveying executive authority, structural security, and institutional clarity.
- **AI Accent (`accent-ai`):** `#7C3AED` — Pure royal violet reserved strictly for AI synthesis, matching engines, automated scoring, and predictive tagging.
- **AI Surface Gradient:** Linear gradient from `#6366F1` to `#8B5CF6` used sparingly for AI recommendation badges, hackathon spotlight cards, and automated insight headers.

### Typography & Content Tokens
- **Text Primary:** `#111827` — Pure deep slate ensuring AAA contrast for body copy and headlines.
- **Text Secondary:** `#667085` — Balanced neutral for secondary metadata, column descriptors, and timestamps.
- **Text Muted:** `#94A3B8` — Tertiary hints, keyboard shortcuts, and disabled states.

### Status & Civic Severity Spectrum
- **Submitted / Informational:** `#2563EB` (Background Tint: `#EFF6FF`, Border: `#BFDBFE`)
- **Under Review / In Evaluation:** `#F59E0B` (Background Tint: `#FFFBEB`, Border: `#FDE68A`)
- **AI Analyzed / Enriched:** `#7C3AED` (Background Tint: `#F5F3FF`, Border: `#DDD6FE`)
- **Approved / Verified / Success:** `#16A34A` (Background Tint: `#F0FDF4`, Border: `#BBF7D0`)
- **High Severity / Critical Bug / Rejection:** `#DC2626` (Background Tint: `#FEF2F2`, Border: `#FECACA`)

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in **Manrope**. Its geometric balance, sturdy letterforms, and modern tech-forward demeanor support dense civic data hierarchies without sacrificing character.

### Hierarchy & Tracking Rules
- **Negative Tracking on Headlines:** All display, headline, and title tiers utilize strict negative letter spacing (`-0.025em` to `-0.01em`) to eliminate floating text and deliver the signature tight, high-impact aesthetic typical of top-tier developer platforms.
- **Labels & Micro-Data:** Metadata labels, hackathon challenge IDs, and category indicators leverage `label-sm` with upper-case transformation and positive tracking (`0.04em`) to ensure instant scannability during rapid judging.
- **Numeric Tabular Alignment:** All quantitative indicators, scores, submission tallies, and timestamps should utilize `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to maintain aligned columns across lists and dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an **8pt modular spacing grid** optimized for high-density multi-pane productivity.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column grid. Outer page margins of `2rem` (32px), inner gutters of `1.5rem` (24px). Accommodates persistent left navigation, flexible data feed, and contextual right-hand AI sidecar panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column grid. Outer margins `1.5rem` (24px), gutters `1.25rem` (20px). Collapses multi-pane sidebars into sliding drawers or expandable bottom sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid. Margins `1rem` (16px), gutters `1rem` (16px). Cards span 4 full columns; multi-step evaluations shift to single-column swipeable vertical stacks.

### Density Tiers
- **Comfortable (Landing / Discovery Mode):** Card gaps at `space-lg` (24px), internal card padding at `space-lg` (24px).
- **Compact (Judging Matrix / Problem Statement Registry):** Row gaps at `space-sm` (8px), table padding at `space-sm` (8px) vertical by `space-md` (16px) horizontal for scanning hundreds of submissions rapidly.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **low-contrast borders combined with ultra-diffused atmospheric shadows**, keeping the canvas clean, crisp, and high-performance.

### Elevation Architecture
- **Elevation 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#F7F8FC`. No shadow.
- **Elevation 1 (Cards, Problem Rows, Content Modules):**
  - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.02)`
- **Elevation 2 (Card Hover, Popovers, Dropdowns):**
  - Border: 1px solid `#CBD5E1`
  - Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`
  - Transition: `all 150ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`
- **Elevation 3 (Slide-over Drawers, Modals, Command Menus):**
  - Border: 1px solid `#E2E8F0`
  - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.05)`

### AI Surface Depth
AI insight containers receive a specialized ambient glow:
- Border: 1px solid rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.25)
- Box Shadow: `0 0 20px -3px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(17, 24, 39, 0.03)`
- Background: Linear gradient from `#FFFFFF` to `#FAF8FF`

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced, tailored geometric radius that reflects contemporary web software:

### Corner Radius System
- **Cards, Modals & Main Containers (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`):** Configured at `12px` to `16px`. Standard problem cards, mentor dossiers, and analytic charts use `14px` (`0.875rem`) for an ergonomic yet technical frame.
- **Inputs, Buttons & Interactive Elements (`rounded-md`):** Fixed at `8px` (`0.5rem`) to maintain sharp click targets and visual balance alongside standard icons.
- **Badges, Status Indicators & AI Glow Pills (`rounded-full`):** Full continuous pill radius (`9999px`), ensuring distinction from structural data cards.
- **Inner Nested Elements:** Always respect outer radius subtraction (`inner_radius = outer_radius - padding`) to ensure optical consistency without awkward border clipping.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#3155D9` background, white text, 8px radius, height 40px (desktop) / 36px (compact), 1px solid transparent. Hover: `#2546BE`. Focus: 2px ring `#3155D9` with 2px offset.
- **AI Accent Action:** Linear gradient (`#6366F1` to `#8B5CF6`), white text, 8px radius. Hover: subtle brightness boost (`108%`) and `0 0 12px rgba(124, 58, 237, 0.35)` glow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, text `#111827`. Hover: `#F8FAFC` and border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Background transparent, text `#667085`. Hover: `#F1F5F9`, text `#111827`.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Shape & Dimensions:** Fully rounded pills (`rounded-full`), height 24px, padding 4px 10px, typography `label-sm`.
- **Status Styles:**
  - *Submitted:* Background `#EFF6FF`, border 1px solid `#BFDBFE`, text `#1D4ED8`.
  - *Under Review:* Background `#FFFBEB`, border 1px solid `#FDE68A`, text `#B45309`.
  - *AI Analyzed:* Background `#F5F3FF`, border 1px solid `#DDD6FE`, text `#6D28D9`, accompanied by a 6px pulsating violet indicator dot or sparkling AI icon.
  - *Approved / Completed:* Background `#F0FDF4`, border 1px solid `#BBF7D0`, text `#15803D`.
  - *High Severity:* Background `#FEF2F2`, border 1px solid `#FECACA`, text `#B91C1C`.

### Cards & Civic Problem Dossiers
- **Base Style:** `#FFFFFF` background, 14px border radius, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, padding `1.5rem` (`24px`).
- **Interaction:** Hover lifts card by `-2px` with an updated shadow transition and active border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Internal Structure:** Top row features Civic Domain tag + Severity/Status Chip; middle displays problem statement heading and 2-line summary; bottom row displays university affiliation, industry mentor avatar stacks, and match readiness indicator.

### Input Fields & Filter Bars
- **Text Inputs:** Height 40px, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, 8px border radius, text `#111827`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, padding `0 12px`. Focus: border `#3155D9`, ring 2px `rgba(49, 85, 217, 0.15)`.
- **Search & Query Filters:** Prefixed with search iconography, equipped with a right-aligned `⌘K` or `Ctrl+K` keycap indicator in `label-sm`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Form Controls:** 16px × 16px, 4px border radius (checkbox) or circular (radio), 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Checked state: `#3155D9` with crisp white check glyph. Focus ring matches input style.

### Lists & Evaluation Tables
- **Data Table:** Flat `#FFFFFF` wrapper, 12px border radius, 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Header row with `#F8FAFC` fill, uppercase `label-sm` text in `#667085`. Alternating rows with clean bottom dividers `#F1F5F9`. Hover state on rows: `#F8FAFC`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **AI Recommendation Banner:** Full-width container with 1px border gradient (`#DDD6FE` to `#E2E8F0`), surface `#FAF5FF`, left-bordered with a 3px vertical purple bar `#7C3AED`. Displays semantic problem-solution similarity scores and recommended corporate mentors.
- **Civic Milestone Progress Stepper:** Segmented pipeline with 4px height connection bars showing stage progression (Discovery -> AI Triage -> University Hack -> Mentor Review -> Implementation).
