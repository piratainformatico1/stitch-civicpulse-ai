Problem Context & Ground Truth

Report ID: #RNC-2024-WT982

During the monsoon and post-monsoon cycles in the Angara block (22km outside Ranchi city proper), local primary health sub-centers reported a 34% surge in waterborne gastrointestinal illnesses within an 18-day window. Field spot-checks conducted by local volunteers and panchayat sevaks discovered critical turbidity spikes and industrial tailings contamination seeping into unlined borehole aquifers.

Traditional municipal testing requires dispatching water sample vials to the Ranchi Central Laboratory with turnaround times spanning 11 to 14 days. By the time contamination advisories are issued, localized handpumps have already distributed contaminated water to over 500 households.

Low-power continuous submersible probe capable of measuring pH (0-14 range, ±0.1 accuracy), Turbidity (0-1000 NTU via nephelometric IR), and Electrical Conductivity for TDS conversion. Must withstand silt buildup for 30 consecutive days without mechanical intervention.

Sub-GHz LoRa transceiver (865-867 MHz India band) transmitting hourly telemetry packets over a minimum 5km radial terrain to the Angara Panchayat Bhavan gateway. Must feature automatic offline buffering for up to 14 days during local cellular gateway blackouts.
